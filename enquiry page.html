Service Enquiry

A WFP service enquiry typically refers to the formal process by which humanitarian partners, governments or registered vendors request information about or access to the WFP's specialised logistics and support services .Because te WFP operates the world's largest humanitarian supply chain, it offers a service marrketplace where other organisations can buy into its infrastructure such as the United Nations HHumanitarian Air Service (UNHAS),fuel provision, ocean freight, warehousing and emergency telecommunications.

an enquiry is the first step in this partnership, allowing an entity to ask for cost estimates, service availability in specific conflict or disaster zones and technical specifications to ensure that life-saving aid reaches its destination efficiently without every small NGO having to build their own global transport network from scratch.

aspects of a WFP service enquiry

Target Audience: Usually humanitarian organisations (NGO's), UN agencies and governments rather than individual citizens.

Service Scope: Covers everything from last mile tracking in remote areas to high-level engineering and IT support in emergency zones.
