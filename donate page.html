Why Your Gift Matters

Every donation, regardless of size, adds to our ability to innovate and provide free resources to the global community. Here is how your support translates into action:

  • Direct Operational Costs (93%) This covers the transfer of aid including the actual cost of food commodities, ocean freight, warehousing and the last-mile trucking required to reach beneficiaries. It also funds implementation activities like beneficiary registration and monitoring.
  • Cash Assistance Roughly 35% of recent expenditures have shifted toward cash and vouchers which empowers local economies and gives families more choice in their nutrition.
  • Resilience and Development Beyond immediate famine relief, funds are used for food for assets programs were communities receive assistance in exchange for working on local infrastructure like irrigaton systems or roads.
