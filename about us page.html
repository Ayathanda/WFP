Who we are

The World Food Programme was founded in 1961 by the United Nations and the Food and Agriculture Organisation it started as an experimental program and became a permanent organisation in 1965. The World Food Programme is now the world's biggest humanitarian agency addressing malnutriton, hunger and food security. It was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2020 for its effort for providing food aids that build resilience.

Our Mission

The World Food Programme's misson is to end global hunger and ensure that all people have access to nutritious food.

Our Vision

A world with zero hunger where everyone has access to safe and sufficient food.

Our Target Audience

Vulnerable populations, governments and policymakers, donors and partner organisations, volunteers and global supporters
