The world's largest humanitarian organisation.

Children go to bed without food.

The World Food Programme provides life-saving food assistance in emergencies, delivering food, cash and vouchers also building long-term resilience through nutrition programs, school meals and climate risk management.

Vaccine Delivery

World Food Programme works with partners like the World Health Organisation (WHO) and governments to deliver vaccines, especially in hard-to-reach or conflict-affected areas. This includes transporting vaccines, cold chain storage, and managing supply chains to ensure vaccines remain potent.
