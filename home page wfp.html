Our History

The World Food Programme (WFP) was founded in 1961 by the United Nations and the Food and Agriculture Organistion, it started as an experimental program and became a permanent organisation in 1965.The World Food Programme is now te world's biggest humanitarian agency addressing malnutrition, hunger and food security. It was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2020 for its effort for providing food aids that build resilience.

Call To Action

The World Food Programme is calling for urgent action and increased funding to combat record, hih global food security driven by climate shocks, conflict and economic crisis with over 300 million people in need.
